## Research Interests

My research interests lie in networked and distributed systems, with a focus on the design and implementation of efficient and scalable protocols and architectures. I am particularly interested in using hardware-software co-design to fundamentally change how data center networks serve applications such as consensus protocols and large language models, enabling more efficient and predictable performance.
