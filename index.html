About Me

Hi, I am Yuke 🙂

Currently, I am a PhD student at Max Planck Institute for Informatics under the supervision of Professor Yiting Xia, focusing on Data Center Networks and Distributed Systems, including network architectures, protocols, measurement, simulation, and network for AI.

Before this, I received my Master's degree in Computer Science from Fudan University supervised by Professor Yang Chen in 2025 and my Dual Bachelor's degree in Computer Science and Japanese from Dalian University of Technology in 2020.

I have 2 years (2020 - 2022) experience of being an full-time Embedded Software Engineer at TP-LINK Technologies, where I contributed to design and implement the software systems of TP-LINK NVR-1008H and the TP-LINK robot operating system.
